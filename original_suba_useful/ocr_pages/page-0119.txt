But if they come harping to me on the theme that it does not
need these instruments to perform its functions, I shall harp
to them on the theme that the inmates of the Quinze Vingts
Hospital for the Blind must be whipped for pretending not
to see a thing.’

‘But,’ I said to him, ‘if our souls were to die, as I see you
wish to conclude, the Resurrection to which we look forward
would be nothing but an illusion, for God would have to
create them all over again—and that would not be resurrec-
tion.’

He interrupted me with a toss of his head. ‘Eh? What’s all
this, in heaven’s name?’ he cried. ‘Who has been putting you
to sleep with that fairy story? What! You? What! Me?
What! My chambermaid? Brought to life?’

‘It is not,’ I replied, ‘a story made up for amusement: it is
an indubitable truth, which I will prove to you.’

‘And J,’ he said, ‘will prove the contrary to you.
