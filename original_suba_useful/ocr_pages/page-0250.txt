‘I was very worried about what device I could employ to
escape from all these waters, which must surely engulf me.
But one of my lovers, who was known as Green-eyes, advised
me to tear out my heart and embark upon it. I should not be
afraid of it being too small, since I had found a place in it for
so many others, nor of it sinking to the bottom, since it was so
light. All I needed to beware of was fire, because the substance
of this vessel was highly inflammable. I should therefore set
sail upon the sea of his tears; the blindfold of his love would
serve me as a Sail and the favourable winds of his sighs, despite
the storming of his rivals, would bring me safely to port.

‘I spent a long time wondering how I could put this enter-
prise into operation. The timidity natural to my sex re-
strained my daring, but finally I decided that if the thing were
not possible, no man would be so insane as to advise it, least of
all a lover to his mistress, and I plucked up courage.
